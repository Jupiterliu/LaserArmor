# Third-party implementations

This repository contains inference-oriented model definitions adapted from the research implementations of Restormer, Uformer, NAFNet, AnyIR, TSFormer, ShiftLUT, MPMF-Net, DCMGFormer, JarvisIR, and GenDeg. It also contains detector implementations corresponding to LaserGuard and BlindedByBeam. Users must comply with the licenses and citation requirements of the original projects and papers.

The project-specific additions are StripeDet, StripeRes-DominantRemove, StripeRes-ResidualRemove, the unified pipeline, the data synthesis utility, and the evaluation utility.
